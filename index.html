Logistics Platform

Pass & Pay — Truck Booking & Goods Transport Platform

A production-quality logistics marketplace connecting shippers with truck owners across India — inspired by the WheelsEye model. Supports full-load and shared part-load bookings with proportional fare splitting, live shipment tracking, payments with GST invoices, and a fully content-managed admin panel.

  • India-wide location search — type-ahead autocomplete over city/district/PIN data, built to scale to the full ~155k-row India Post dataset.
  • Smart fare engine — live estimates from base + distance + GST with long-haul surcharges; shared loads split cost by weight.
  • CMS-driven admin — non-technical staff manage banners, offers, FAQs, KYC, bookings, and payments from a grouped django-unfold dashboard.
  • Role-based accounts — shipper / transporter / admin flows with live tracking timelines and printable invoices.
